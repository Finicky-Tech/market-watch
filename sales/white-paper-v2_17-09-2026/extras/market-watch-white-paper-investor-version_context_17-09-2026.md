## Key decisions made

- Original paper's target reader = NBS officials, legislators, policy
  analysts — technical, credibility-conscious, institutional. That drove:
  precedent-led argument (no live product data yet), a sequenced
  validate→partner→integrate "ask," honest flagging of unresolved design
  questions (unit normalization, submission verification) as a deliberate
  credibility signal, and a brief/secondary business-value section.
- Register for that NBS paper was calibrated to "non-technical but
  policy-literate" — plain language, Finicky Technologies GitHub Pages
  profile register (see ways-of-working.md: "plain English, active voice,
  no jargon, benefit-led framing, calibrated to a senior non-technical
  Nigerian professional readership"). New investor version should match
  this or go one level simpler — i.e. even less institutional/technical
  framing, more personal and outcome-oriented.
- New version's audience is explicitly NOT NBS — it's personal network
  investors (uncles/aunties, past/current bosses). The core "ask" section
  (validate → partner → integrate with NBS) is still relevant as the
  business's path to validation/traction, but should likely be reframed as
  "why this business will work and where it's headed" rather than a
  formal institutional appeal — this reframing itself is not yet decided,
  just flagged as the obvious tension to resolve when drafting.
- All source URLs from the original paper must be preserved/available for
  the new version (see Source material below) — reuse the same evidentiary
  backbone (FPCA/JRC validation study, NBS CPI facts, precedent landscape)
  since accuracy matters even in a simplified version; only the *framing*
  and *level of detail* should simplify, not the underlying facts.
